Five things that get a coin sold

You pick them per coin. Any one that fires is enough. Nothing else — a red candle, a rug rumour, a quiet chart — makes Argus touch your bag.

Dev sells any amount
The creator wallet sells > 0 tokens. Size doesn't matter; the first sell is the signal.
Dev transfers tokens out
Tokens leave the creator wallet to any address. Usually the step right before a sell from a fresh wallet.
Bundle cluster sells 10% of its bag
Wallets that bought in the creation block are tracked as one bag. When ≥ 10% of that bag is sold, it fires.
A top-10 holder dumps 25%
Any wallet in the top 10 sells ≥ 25% of what it holds in one go or within one minute.
Creator fees claimed and sold
The creator-fee wallet claims and swaps to SOL. Fine once in a while; Argus fires when the swap follows the claim within 60 s.

Toggles here are a preview of the per-coin settings. Defaults when you add a coin: the first three on.

How the exit works

Four steps, every slot, for every coin you hold. Timings are targets, not promises — Solana decides the block.

Watch
Argus streams every transaction touching your coins and the wallets it has flagged, slot by slot.

Detect
A sell or transfer from a flagged wallet is matched against the triggers you set for that coin.
target < 400 ms after the block

Bundle
A Jupiter swap for 100% (or your set %) is signed from the agent wallet and wrapped in a Jito bundle with a tip.
tip 0.001 – 0.01 SOL, scales with size
4
Exit
The bundle lands and your SOL is in the agent wallet. Argus writes the event to your log.
target same or next block
